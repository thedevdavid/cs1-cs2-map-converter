# PO terrain layers v2

Generated by `src/po-offline.cs` from the Workshop map's PO data and the CS1 heightmap `DakotaFalls.png`. Only **terrain-like** objects (see `../po-classes.md`); structures, waterfall sheets, decals and zero-size emitters are in no v2 raster.

## Encoding

All PNGs: 16-bit grey, row 0 = north, **value = metres × 32** (65535 = 2048 m), 0 = no object. Per cell the maximum over the layer's objects (no single-object winner).

- `*-cs1-1081.png`: CS1 frame, 1081 × 1081 samples at x = −8640 + col·16, z = 8640 − row·16 (the CS1 heightmap vertex grid; covers the exterior ring).
- `*-cs2-4096.png`: CS2 playable frame, 4096 × 4096 pixels of 3.5 m over ±7168 m, sampled at pixel centres x = −7168 + (col + 0.5)·3.5, z = 7168 − (row + 0.5)·3.5, rasterised directly from the full-resolution triangles.
- Both: every triangle is rasterised at the samples (barycentric y), and every vertex is also splatted to its nearest sample so thin walls count.
- v1 note: `../po-offline-heights.png` used value = metres × 64 and so clipped everything above 1 024 m; v2 does not clip below 2 048 m.

## Rules

Per object, on a 4 m lattice: footprint A = covered samples × 16 m²; terrain = bilinear CS1 heightmap; **max added** = max over samples of (object top − terrain); **floating** = share of samples whose underside is more than 5 m above the terrain.

- **Spike**: max added > 2 × √A (the spike limit column) → removed: a column much taller than it is wide. Applies to every terrain-like subclass.
- **Overhang**: floating > 50 % → removed for rock work and falls steps; **kept** for mountains, which the max-per-cell raster fills solid down to the terrain anyway.
