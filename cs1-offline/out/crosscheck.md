| cells only offline, by owner class | structure/paved block 133 |

Why the rasters differ: the geometry is identical (section 1), the sampling rule is not. Replicated on the in-game full meshes:

| rule (1081 grid, 16 m) | vs in-game raster | vs offline v1 raster |
|---|---|---|
| triangles at sample points only | median 3.779 m, p99 57.478 m | median 0.003 m, p99 28.864 m |
| + every vertex snapped to the nearest sample (offline v1) | median 2.906 m, p99 43.690 m | median 0.003 m, p99 0.005 m |
| + a point every 4 m along every edge, snapped (in-game exporter, `PoGeometry.cs`) | median 0.002 m, p99 0.005 m | median 2.912 m, p99 43.694 m |

So each raster equals its own rule applied to the same meshes (the residue is the 6 unedited objects and the effect emitters, which are not in the mesh file). Orientation and scale agree. Snapping vertices or edge points to the nearest 16 m sample lifts cells on steep flanks by up to hundreds of metres (the hm-rebuild "columns"), so **v2 uses triangles only** (vertices only for objects that cover no sample). The PNGs clip at 1 024 m (× 64); the JSON values do not.

## 4. heights-block vs heights-raw

- block differs from raw in 61684 cells (27254 higher, 34430 lower); |Δ| median 0.50 m, p99 10.02 m, max 61.52 m. raw2 differs from raw in 51 cells.
- **Recommendation**: use **heights-block for the water-surface comparison** (the exporter's water surface is BlockHeights + depth, so only block makes depth = surface − terrain consistent), and keep **heights-raw for stamping**. Block bakes building and network footprints (dams included) into the ground, which in CS2 would be rebuilt as objects or networks and must not be left as terrain bumps or cuts.
- Triangle-count mismatches in section 1 are the 2 vanilla Castle Ruins objects: no asset package offline, so no triangles (their vertices are still exact).
