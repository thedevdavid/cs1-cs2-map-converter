- **Squeeze vs crop: my claim corrected.** hmap-ext has no stage that seeds the exterior with known terrain; the inner is copied and locked, everything outside is synthesized from the inner border. A 1:1 crop would simply discard the CS1 ring and synthesize a plausible one. Seeding the ring would be a new stage about the size of the trend stage (ring at 14 m/px, locked, border samples moved to the ring's outer edge, seam/breach/corridor/erosion fade moved with it), scoped as a v2 item. So from hmap-ext's side the squeeze costs nothing new; its price is horizontal only: slopes in the playable area are 1.2× steeper than CS1 at true heights, and the exterior continues those slopes.
- **Other effects at 1024 m:** sea auto-detection may see more "flat" cells (dimensionless gradient threshold), so check the "sea:" log line and pass `--sea-level none` if it guesses; the map is inland regardless. Headroom passes narrowly (river exits at 37–38 m, far-field floor about 18 m, 10 m check). Clipped peaks become 1024 m and the exterior is capped there. With a crop, border samples would come from the cropped edge; nothing else changes.

Decision stays with you, now with correct costs: keep the squeeze (no hmap-ext work, slopes 1.2× steeper) or crop 1:1 (faithful geometry, ring lost unless a seeding stage is built).
