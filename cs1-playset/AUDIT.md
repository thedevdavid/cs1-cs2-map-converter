### Surface Painter and Extra Landscaping Tools
- Neither Surface Painter (689937287) nor any ELT was enabled when the map was saved, and neither is in the collection.
- CS1 keeps serializable data from mods that are no longer active, though, so earlier painting could survive. Surface Painter is therefore **now enabled** in the playset, so that DakotaExport reads whatever is there.
- Skyve lists ELT 502750307 as Surface Painter's dependency. ELT Revisited 3733110460 meets it: it ships the same `NaturalResourcesBrush.dll` that Surface Painter references.
- Skyve notes that Move It "causes issues with" Surface Painter (tool conflict only).

### Theme mix (2972881605) and its collection (2972871641)
- `ThemeMix.xml` takes atmosphere from Australia Coast (1305157510), Owl's Albedo, Seychelles, r69-coastal (1594406264) and Springwood.
- Structures come from Seychelles. Terrain textures come from Seychelles, Springwood, Owl's Albedo and r69-coastal, plus a grass colour from Los Cabos (2858527761).
- Water comes from CO-Temperate, and the LUT is Relight2Natural (More Relight LUTs, not installed).
- Every theme is installed and in the playset. The CO-Temperate reference uses the removed id 2954236385, so Theme Mixer may fall back to default water textures. That's visual only.
- The author's Render It! settings are on the collection page and saved in `theme/RenderIt-DakotaFalls.txt`.
- Relight values are only in a screenshot on 2972881605.
