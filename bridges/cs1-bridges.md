## Method

- **Bridge segment:** netPrefab AI `RoadBridgeAI`, `TrainTrackBridgeAI` or `PedestrianBridgeAI`, or a name ending in ` B`, ` E`, ` B0`, ` E0`, or containing `Elevated`/`Bridge`. The monorail segments (`Monorail Track - No Pylons`, `Monorail Oneway Track`) are collected as their own decorative group.
- **Chain:** bridge segments joined through nodes that are not on the ground. A node is ground if it has the `OnGround` flag or sits within 1.5 m of the terrain. `NetNode.m_elevation` is not usable for this: it is 0 on the Detroit crossing nodes 93 m above the river.
- **Bridge:** chains of the same class (road, rail, path, monorail) merged when at least 60 % of the shorter chain lies within 45 m of the other (twin one-way interstate carriageways, ramps beside their mainline). 53 bridges result.
- **Heights:** sampled every ~8 m along the Bézier. Above terrain = deck y − `heights-raw.png` (the saved heightmap). Above water = deck y − `water.png` surface on wet cells. "Over water" = length of deck over wet cells. These are CS1 metres; the CS2 x2 set doubles all heights (`net-converter/README.md`, height factor 2), x and z are unchanged.
- **Pillars:** CS1 buildings (top-level and `skippedBuildings`, i.e. node-owned) whose prefab contains `Pillar` or `Pylon` (power pylons excluded) within 15 m of the deck line.
- **Passes over:** other road/rail/ship segments that pass under the deck (within 6 m in plan, deck more than 4 m above them).
- **Importance score:** chain length × max height above terrain / 1000, × 3 when a CS1 bridge model, a PO bridge piece or the monorail arches belong to it. The ranked list below is that score, adjusted by hand for the showcase captions in `audit/web.md`.

## Network Skins on bridge segments

CS1 Network Skins removed the default bridge pillars (`PillarModifier Type=Bridge, Pillar=null`) on 98 segments of BR001, all 8 of BR005, 6 of BR006, 38 of BR008, and a few on BR003/025/030/032/034. These bridges are carried by something else: a CS1 bridge model, PO pieces, hand-placed pillars, or nothing (long free spans). The converter already writes `noPillars` on those edges; the CS2MCP bridge ignores it today.
